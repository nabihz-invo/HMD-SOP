1How a request starts

Every request begins on the HMD website.

  1. The customer adds items to the cart from Buy, Rental or Spare Parts, or opens a Maintenance request.
  2. For Buy, Rental and Spare Parts, they fill in their contact, company, country and industry details at checkout.
  3. Spare Parts also has a Bulk / Quick Order option, where the customer types or uploads a list of parts.
  4. Maintenance uses the Aftersales form. The customer describes the machine and the issue.
  5. A mixed cart is split. One checkout with Buy, Rental and Spare Parts items creates a separate request and a separate deal for each.
The result A deal is created in HubSpot with all the linked information: the customer, the company and the requested items.
The deal form The deal form in HubSpot follows the deal's pipeline. It shows the information needed for that type of deal: Buy, Rental, Spare Parts or Maintenance.

2The four request types

Each type lands in its own HubSpot pipeline. No salesperson approval happens before the deal is created.

Buy

What happensA deal is created with the customer, company and requested items. If prices are available, the Amount is filled in USD.

You checkPrices, items, shipping details and the customer's preferred contact channel.

Rental

What happensA separate deal is created in the Rental pipeline. The request starts as Pending. No price is calculated.

You checkAvailability, rental dates, rental type, estimated hours, requested services and delivery address.

Spare Parts — cart

What happensA deal is created in the Spare Parts pipeline with each part as an item. Any uploaded parts list is attached.

You checkPart codes, quantities, pricing, compatibility, pickup or delivery, carrier and address.

Spare Parts — Bulk / Quick Order

What happensA new deal is created with the parts list and files. Items have no prices.

You checkThe full list, duplicate rows, files, missing prices, and attach the right company.

Maintenance

What happensA deal is created in the Maintenance pipeline with a full request note. There are no item rows.

You checkUrgency, machine model, serial number, the issue, whether the machine is running, service needed and files.

Quotations

The customer can ask to be contacted by WhatsApp, Email or both. WhatsApp is used if they choose nothing.

How quotations are sent Machinery quotations are sent by a separate HubSpot process. When a quotation is sent, a notification arrives to the user.
What you do Open the right deal, review it, and contact the customer to clarify or price the request. Before sending a quotation, check that one has not already gone out.

3What you find on the deal

The website fills these in for you. The deal form shows the fields that match the deal's pipeline.

On the dealWhat to know
Deal nameBuilt from the request, the company and the first item code. Maintenance uses the machine model.
Deal ownerChosen from the customer's country. See section 5.
Close dateSet to 7 days after the request. This is not a delivery or service promise.
Currency and sourceUSD, and lead source "Website".
Order reference and Quotation numberBoth show the website request reference. A filled Quotation number does not mean a quote exists.
AmountFilled for Buy only, when prices are available. Rental, Spare Parts and Maintenance are not priced automatically.
DescriptionThe customer's notes, contact preference, and rental or shipping details. For Maintenance, the full request summary.
Shipping detailsDestination country, port and Incoterms when the customer chose them. Spare Parts also shows Delivery or Pick-up, and Air Freight, Sea Freight or DEAL. A DHL choice on the website shows as DEAL.
ItemsName, quantity, and unit price when there is one. Buy items include machine specifications. Bulk / Quick Order items say "Pricing to be confirmed".
Tuboshu ForLifeShown as two rows: the machine and the coverage. On the coverage row, quantity is the hours and unit price is the hourly rate.
Maintenance detailsSupport level, priority, machine model, serial number, operating hours, ForLife membership, issue type, issue start date, running status, service needed, preferred time and file links.
Look in the notes too Some details are saved as notes, not fields: rental dates, type, hours and services; part serial numbers and pickup branch; uploaded file links; and the Maintenance summary. If a Maintenance field is empty, the answer is usually in the summary.
What you do Check the deal fields and the notes. Confirm every requested item is there and priced correctly. An empty field does not mean the customer left it blank.

4Contact and company

The website links each deal to a contact and, in most cases, a company.

  1. Contacts are matched by email only. The same person using a different email becomes a new contact.
  2. Cart and Maintenance requests create the contact if it is new, or update the existing one with the latest name, phone, company and country.
  3. Companies are matched by name. An existing company is reused as it is. A new company is created as a Prospect, with the same owner as the deal.

6Sending the deal to SAP

SAP is linked to HubSpot. Moving a deal to Order Confirmation creates the quote in SAP.

  1. Check the customer and company, items, quantities, agreed prices, currency and delivery details.
  2. Set the Source company field. The quote is created under the company selected there. Metamac is the default.
  3. Move the deal stage to Order Confirmation.
  4. The quote is created in SAP automatically, using the deal's information and its source company.
What you do Review the deal, confirm the Source company is right, then move the deal to Order Confirmation.

7Order updates from SAP

SAP sends each order status change to HubSpot automatically.

  1. When the order status changes in SAP, HubSpot is updated through a webhook. You do nothing.
  2. The current status appears in the card at the top right of the deal page.
What you do Open the deal and read the order status card at the top right before you update the customer.

8If something goes wrong

An error does not mean nothing was saved. Always check before asking the customer to submit again.

SituationWhat you do
Customer sees a "required field" or submission errorHelp them complete the missing information. If the details are correct and the error stays, report it.
Part of a mixed cart failsCheck each request and deal first. Other parts may already be saved. Do not duplicate a part that worked.
Bulk / Quick Order saved, but the customer is told to contact HMDFind the reference, look for an existing deal, and ask the administrator to complete what is missing.
Maintenance deal created with a warningUse the existing deal. The details are in the Description or summary. Ask the administrator to fix the missing note or field. Do not create a second request.
Items, notes or contact links look incompleteCompare the customer's request with the deal before quoting or confirming. Not every failure shows a warning.
Customer lost connection or got no confirmationSearch by email, company and recent request details before they resubmit. This avoids duplicates.
SAP handover or order creation failsCheck the order status card on the deal, then contact the HubSpot / SAP administrator. Do not move the stage again until they confirm.

When you report a problem

  1. Record the request reference, deal reference, request type, time of submission and the error message shown.
  2. Check what was already created.
  3. Send the details to the website / HubSpot administrator or the SAP administrator.
What you do Keep the reference and the error. Check for existing records. Repair the existing request instead of creating a new one.
