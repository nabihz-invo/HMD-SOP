    The big picture

    Every request starts on the HMD website, lives as a deal in HubSpot, and turns into documents in SAP. Most of the movement between them is automatic.

    A deal's life in one line

    The names change by pipeline, but every deal follows the same shape.

    Request on website→Deal in HubSpot→Quote / PI→Client confirms→Sales order in SAP→Payment→Delivered · Closed Won
    Any stage→Closed Lost→Open SAP documents closed
    Three rules for everyone
    • The deal is the single source of truth. Check it before you call the client. SAP updates arrive on it by themselves.
    • Never create a second deal or a second PI for the same request. Repair or amend the existing one.
    • Move stages only as this guide says. Some stage moves send emails to the client or create documents in SAP.

    Common to every pipeline

    From the website to a HubSpot deal

    What the customer submits, what the website creates for you, and how a deal is set up before anyone touches it.

    How a request starts

    No salesperson approval happens before the deal is created.

    1. The customer adds items from Buy, Rental or Spare Parts to the cart, or opens a Maintenance request.
    2. At checkout they give their contact details, company, country and industry, and how they want to be contacted: WhatsApp, Email or both. WhatsApp is used if they choose nothing.
    3. Spare Parts also offers Bulk / Quick Order: the customer types or uploads a list of parts.
    4. A mixed cart is split. One checkout with Buy, Rental and Spare Parts items creates a separate request and a separate deal for each.
    5. The website creates the deal, links the contact and company, adds the items, and gives the request a reference.
    6. About two minutes later the client gets a welcome message by their preferred channel.
    The deal form The deal form in HubSpot follows the pipeline. It shows only the fields that matter for Machinery, Rental, Spare Parts or Maintenance.

    What the spare parts form asks

    Contact and company

    The website links each deal to a contact and, in most cases, a company.

    1. Contacts are matched by email only. The same person with a different email becomes a new contact.
    2. An existing contact is updated with the latest name, phone, company, country and preferred channel.
    3. Companies are matched by name. An existing company is reused as it is: its owner and type never change.
    4. A new company is created as a Prospect. A new contact gets the company owner, lead source "Website", contact type "Prospect" and status "Trying to contact".
    Preferred channel belongs to the contact If the same client orders again and picks a different channel, the new choice is used from then on, for all their deals.

    Documents and order status

    You never upload SAP documents to HubSpot. They open from the deal.

    1. Open the deal. The order card at the top right shows the current SAP status and every document for the deal.
    2. Click View next to a document: proforma invoice, sales order, invoice, delivery note or payment receipt.
    3. The document opens in HMD's design for that company. To send or keep a copy, use the browser's Print → Save as PDF.
    Good to know
    • The Proforma Invoice (PI) is the SAP Sales Quotation. The PI number on the deal is that quotation number.
    • Client emails carry a link to the PI or receipt, not an attachment.
    • Every change to an open PI in SAP updates the deal's lines and total automatically.

    Spare Parts pipeline

    The most automated pipeline. The parts team prices a draft PI, HubSpot sends it, reminds, follows the order, payment and delivery, and closes what goes silent.

    Who is involved

    Each role has a clear part. The salesperson is the only contact with the client.

    Deal owner (salesperson)

    The only person who talks to the client. Answers the pricing team's questions, follows up the PI, agrees changes, moves the deal to Order Confirmation.

    Parts pricing team (Parts Executives)

    Prices and amends the PI in SAP for its entity.

    • Nigeria and FZE: HMD Nigeria Maintenance & Spare Parts
    • Ghana: HMD Ghana Maintenance & Spare Parts
    • Guinea and Metamac: Rania El Khoury for now

    Head of Sales

    Rania El Khoury for now. Receives the 48-hour pricing escalation and decides on credit line exemptions.

    SAP team, finance and warehouse

    Convert the PI to a sales order, re-check stock and credit, register payments against the PI number, issue the invoice and the delivery note.

    Golden rules

    • Website only. Every parts request comes through the website, so it has a reference and a deal.
    • One request, one PI. Always amend the same PI in SAP. Never create a new one.
    • The salesperson is the only contact with the client. The pricing team asks through the Pricing question field.
    • The client pays against the PI number, not the website reference. C1, C3 and C6 tell them so.
    • An alternative part is marked on the PI next to the part number the client asked for.
    • Do not move a deal to Quote Sent by hand. Set PI status to Ready to send and let HubSpot send the PI.

    When something looks wrong

    Help and glossary

    An error does not mean nothing was saved. Always check before asking the client to submit again.

    If something goes wrong

    SituationWhat you do
    Client sees a "required field" or submission errorHelp them complete the missing information. If the details are right and the error stays, report it.
    Part of a mixed cart failsCheck each request and deal first. Other parts may already be saved. Do not duplicate a part that worked.
    Bulk / Quick Order saved, but the client is told to contact HMDFind the reference, look for an existing deal, and ask Invocom to complete what is missing.
    Maintenance deal created with a warningUse the existing deal. The details are in the description or summary. Do not create a second request.
    Client lost connection or got no confirmationSearch by email, company and recent request before they resubmit.
    No PI number on a spare parts deal after a few minutesCheck the Entity Name and the line items are there, then report it to Invocom.
    PI changed in SAP but the deal total did notMake sure the change was made on the same PI, and it is still open. Report it if the deal stays out of date.
    Client did not receive the PI email (C3)Check PI status is Sent and the client's email on the contact is right. Send the PI link from the deal's order card if needed.
    Wrong HMD company on the dealFix the Entity Name before the PI or sales order is created. If one already exists, ask the SAP team.
    SAP order creation failsCheck the order card on the deal, then contact Invocom or the SAP administrator. Do not move the stage again until they confirm.

    When you report a problem

    1. Note the request reference, deal link, pipeline, time and the error shown.
    2. Check what was already created: contact, company, deal, PI.
    3. Send the details to Invocom (website and HubSpot) or the SAP administrator.
    What you do Keep the reference and the error. Repair the existing deal instead of creating a new one.
